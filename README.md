### 🧠 Architectural Philosophy & Approach
I operate at the intersection of **tacit builder intuition** (shipping real systems from the bare metal up) and **explicit systems engineering** (formalizing architecture, cybersecurity, and deterministic verification). 

My work is driven by a core loop:
$$\text{Intent} \longrightarrow \text{Model} \longrightarrow \text{Spec} \longrightarrow \text{Build} \longrightarrow \text{Verify}$$

Every system should be explainable, testable, and built with explicit boundaries where human authority remains absolute.
